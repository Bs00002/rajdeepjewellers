Our Story & Heritage

A journey of trust, generational craftsmanship, and dedication to timeless gold and silver ornaments in Kutch, Gujarat.

THE RAJDEEP LEGACY

Three Decades of Purity and Craftsmanship

Founded in 1994, Rajdeep Jewellers (Rajdeep Gold Palace) was born out of a profound passion for preserving authentic Indian jewellery traditions. Located in the heart of Bhachau at Nagarpalika Shopping Center-1, our showroom has grown alongside the generations of families who trust us for life's most precious occasions.

Led by Rajesh Patadiya and Piyush Patadiya, our philosophy is anchored in transparency, unwavering gold purity, and personalized consultation for every bride, groom, and jewellery lover.
