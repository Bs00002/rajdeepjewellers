Our Story & Heritage

A journey of trust, generational craftsmanship, and dedication to timeless gold and silver ornaments in Kutch, Gujarat.

THE RAJDEEP LEGACY

Where Tradition Meets Timeless Jewellery

Rajdeep Jewellers brings together traditional Indian jewellery and contemporary elegance for customers in Bhachau and across Kutch. Our collections include gold and silver jewellery, lightweight Rajputana-inspired designs, traditional ornaments and jewellery for special occasions.

From everyday elegance to wedding celebrations, every piece is chosen to carry a story.

Established in 1994, our showroom is located at Nagarpalika Shopping Center-1 in Bhachau. We take pride in being part of the wider jewellery community in Kutch, serving over 500+ customer families with honest guidance, authentic craftsmanship, and dedicated personal service.
